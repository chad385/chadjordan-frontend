# Origin story — why the work is built the way it is

Removed from `proof/case-studies.md` on 2026-10-24, deliberately. It was the
public origin story for the old "Compound" positioning (own vs. rent). It's
still true, and it still explains why everything is built by hand on
infrastructure the client owns. It just isn't the sales argument anymore, so
the content engine shouldn't build pieces around it.

---

I built a product on a major cloud platform. It worked. Then the platform
switched it off — twice — and the work was gone. No recourse, no ownership.
I'd built on someone else's ground, and they were within their rights to erase
it. I don't build that way now, and I don't let my clients either.

(When telling it: say "a major cloud platform," never the name. Tell it as
conviction, not complaint.)
