# Competitive Landscape

## Market Position
Chad Jordan · Studio occupies a position nobody else fully holds: **genuine ownership + a full operating system + creative-director taste, done-for-you.** Everyone can get the same free engine; the studio is the part that isn't free.

## Competitor Categories

### Direct Competitors
| Competitor | Their Strength | Their Weakness | Our Advantage |
|-----------|---------------|----------------|---------------|
| GoHighLevel resellers | Turnkey CRM/funnels, recurring model | It's "your own software" you're actually renting — a sub-account, not the deed | Real ownership; you hold it outright |
| "Stop renting your website" WordPress shops | Cheap, own-your-site message | Commodity, site-only, no brand or system | A full operating system, made unmistakable |
| Federation / starter-template clones | Same open-source engine, fast to stand up | Same starter, no eye — everyone looks alike | The director and the taste; nobody mistakes it for another |

### Indirect Competitors (DIY / Adjacent)
- Rolling your own open-source stack — free, but you become your own IT department.
- Squarespace / Wix / Framer — a prettier rental you still don't own.
- Hiring a freelancer per piece — scattered across platforms nobody owns.

## Market Gaps We Fill
1. Ownership *and* taste in one place — the OSS crowd has ownership without the eye; the agencies have the eye without the ownership.
2. A commissioned brand on infrastructure the client holds outright — not a template, not a rental, not an IT project.

- Independent build/creator communities
- Around the campaign/credibility work (NASA × RL, Durindal, G-Shock, Porsche)
