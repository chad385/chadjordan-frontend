# Positioning — Chad Jordan Studio

**Core:** Chad Jordan does brand work for expert-led firms whose presentation doesn't match who they actually are. The firm is good. The people who already work with it know that. Everyone else is judging it from a website, a deck, and a logo that make it look smaller and newer than it is.

**The promise:** stop looking smaller than you are. The work is a correction. Nothing gets inflated or invented. The brand is rebuilt around what is already true, so the first impression matches the firm a client meets six months in.

**Who it's for:** founders and partners of expert-led firms: advisory, consulting, professional services, specialist B2B, defense and technical firms, founder-led studios. They sell judgment and expertise. Their clients stay once they're in. The problem is getting strangers to the first meeting with the right impression.

**The gap:** the distance between what a company is and how it reads. Strangers make up their minds in about ninety seconds, from whatever the firm has put in front of them. When the presentation is weaker than the firm, those ninety seconds go against it.

**What the gap costs:**
- Losing work to competitors who are worse at the job but look more established.
- Over-explaining in every pitch to make up for a first impression that undersold the firm.
- Discounting to compensate, and getting priced as a commodity instead of as the expert.
- Borrowed language: positioning copied from bigger firms because it felt safer, which makes the firm sound like everyone else.

**How the work runs:** find the dissonance between what the firm says and what it is. Dig for what's actually true, through a long conversation rather than a questionnaire. Cut until only that's left. Then build the identity, image world, site, and system around it so the team can live inside it.

**Who's doing it:** twenty years directing brand for companies with budgets (national broadcast campaigns, Webby-honored digital platforms, $2B+ brand revenue scale), applied to firms that can't normally get that level of creative direction.

**Build principle (mention in passing, never argue it):** everything is built by hand, and the client owns what gets built: the site, the files, the infrastructure. This is how the work is done. It is never the topic of a piece and never the reason to hire the studio.
