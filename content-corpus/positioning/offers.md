# Offers Architecture

## Value Ladder

Three ways in — lowest commitment to highest. The AI uses this to match CTAs to content topics.

### Tier 1: Entry
**The Strategic Session** — $1,500 (credited toward what comes next)
- What they get: 90 minutes, one decision, a one-page brief within 48 hours.
- Who it's for: a founder who knows something's wrong but not what to build first.
- CTA: `/#session` → "Start with one decision →"

### Tier 2: The Build
**The Compound** — by commission *(floor still to set — circling $15k)*
- What they get: the commissioned build — brand, content, and owned infrastructure, made unmistakable. You hold the deed.
- Who it's for: a founder ready to stop renting and own the whole operating system.
- CTA: `/#session` (opens with the Strategic Session as the front door)

### Tier 3: The Recurring Layer
**The Standing Engagement** — monthly *(rate still to set — circling $25–40k core)*
- What they get: the part most people skip — I keep the content coming and the Compound compounding, month over month.
- Who it's for: a founder who wants the asset to keep growing instead of going stale.
- CTA: named in the Compound handoff; not a public checkout.

> **STILL TO SET:** the Compound commission floor and the Standing Engagement monthly rate. Decide the public floor before these numbers ship.

## Offer-to-Content Mapping

| Content Topic | Best CTA |

| Own-vs-rent, platform risk, data ownership | The Strategic Session |
| Brand systems, taste, "unmistakable," case studies | The Compound |
