# Offers — Chad Jordan Studio

Three ways to work together. Use these exact names. "The Compound" is retired and must never appear as an offer.

**1. The Strategic Session — $1,500**
Ninety minutes on one decision. A one-page brief in the client's hands within 48 hours. The fee is credited toward whatever comes next. Best for: a founder who knows something is off and wants a clear read before committing to a build. The right CTA for most articles.

**2. The Commission — by commission**
The full build: the brand brain, identity, image world, site, and system, made so the team can live inside it.
- The brand brain: everything true about the firm, written down — voice, positioning, proof, the words it will never say. The document every other asset is built from, and the one almost nobody has.
- Identity: logo, type, color, and the rules for using them.
- Image world: a visual DNA locked once, then applied for close to nothing per image after the first.
- The site: built by hand as a commissioned piece, on infrastructure the client owns.
- The system: content, capture, and the quiet automation that keeps it running.
Best for: a firm that has outgrown its presentation and is ready to fix it properly.

**3. The Standing Engagement — monthly**
Ongoing work after the build: keeping the brand running and growing month over month so it doesn't go stale. Best for: firms that want the brand maintained by the person who built it.

**CTA guidance:** the default next step is "Start a conversation" at /contact. Match the offer to the article: diagnosis and decision topics → Strategic Session; identity, voice, image, or site topics → The Commission.
