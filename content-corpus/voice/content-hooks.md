# Content Hooks

Proven opening patterns that grab attention. The AI selects from these when writing articles. All bend toward the own-vs-rent frame.

## Contrarian Statement
- "You don't have a marketing problem. You have a landlord problem."
- "A prettier rental is still a rental."
- "The template everyone praises is the reason you look like everyone."

## The Own-vs-Rent Reframe
- "Rent compounds against you. A Compound compounds *for* you."
- "You can rent a beautiful business. You just can't keep it."
- "Every month you pay a platform, you're building their asset, not yours."

## Specific Number + Unexpected Result
- "Ten years of rent buys you exactly nothing. One build buys you the deed."
- "$500 a month across a typical stack. Watch where that line ends."

## Direct Challenge
- "If your platform doubled its price tomorrow, what would you do?"
- "Stop renting your existence from a company that can switch you off."

## Story Opening
- "A decade with a real estate license taught me what ownership is actually worth. Then I started building it in digital form."
- "A founder came to me with real IP and a site they kept rebuilding themselves. It never did them justice. They didn't need a prettier rental."
