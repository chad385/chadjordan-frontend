# Tone Examples

Real examples of the brand voice in action. The AI uses these to calibrate tone.

## Homepage / Manifesto (Terse + Declarative)
> "Your rent compounds against you. Your *Compound* compounds for you."
>
> "A Compound is your whole business behind your own gates — the brand, the content, the CRM, and the infrastructure it runs on — on ground you own, not rent."
>
> "Same materials everyone has. The difference is who's directing — and who holds the deed."

## Blog / Essay Opening (Authoritative + Concrete)
> "Every month on a rented platform is money out that builds someone else's asset. Every month in your Compound builds yours. Give it a few years and the gap is the whole point."
>
> "You can rent a beautiful business. You just can't keep it. The day you stop paying, it's gone — the template, the list, the data, all of it."

## Email (Direct + Personal)
> "Quick one: if the platform your business runs on doubled its price tomorrow, what would you actually do? Most founders can't answer that. That's the problem."

## Social Post (Punchy)
> "You don't have a website problem. You have a landlord problem."
>
> "Everyone can get the engine for free. I'm the part that isn't free."

## The Close (Proof Motif)
> "You don't get a website. You get the deed."
> "Stop renting. Build the Compound."
